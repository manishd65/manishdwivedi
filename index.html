About

My doctoral work sits at the intersection of structural vibrations, nonlinear mechanics, and acoustics — specifically, how coupled structural-acoustic systems behave when driven beyond the linear regime. Over the past four years I've been building analytical and numerical tools (MATLAB, COMSOL, ANSYS) to study these problems in flexible waveguides, alongside teaching courses on vibrations and acoustics.

01 — Focus

Research

Analytical perturbation methods, frequency-domain numerical schemes, and finite element modeling applied to nonlinear wave propagation in acoustic waveguides.

Rigid Cylindrical Duct

Second-order nonlinear wave propagation with uniform mean flow; corrected branch-selection in the dispersion relation and Lagrangian-density pressure reconstruction for the frequency-domain numerical scheme (FDNS).

Rectangular Waveguide

Modal cascade behavior under single-mode vs. multimode excitation; diagonal self-mode resonance proven analytically, cross-modal dominance characterized numerically.

Flexible Waveguides

Coupled fluid–plate eigenproblem with mean flow; multiple-scales amplitude evolution via Fredholm solvability, extended to a bifrequency FDNS formulation.

Methods

Symbolic verification in Maple, numerical implementation in MATLAB, finite element modeling with T6 elements and PML absorbing boundaries.
