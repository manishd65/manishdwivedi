About

Overview

I am a PhD candidate in the Vibro-Acoustics (FRITA) Laboratory at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, working under the supervision of Dr. Venkata R. Sonti. My research focuses on weakly nonlinear acoustic wave propagation in rigid and flexible waveguides under uniform mean flow, using analytical perturbation methods, frequency-domain numerical schemes, and finite element modeling.

Focus Areas

Research

My doctoral work addresses nonlinear acoustic wave propagation and sound-structure interaction in waveguides carrying a uniform mean flow, combining analytical perturbation methods with numerical validation.

Rigid Rectangular Waveguide

Derived the convected wave equation and second-order solutions for self- and cross-mode interactions; proved diagonal self-mode resonance analytically and characterized cross-modal dominance under multimode excitation.

Rigid Cylindrical Waveguide

Extended the second-order analysis to cylindrical geometry; corrected a branch-selection bug in the downstream wavenumber formula and derived conditions for resonance and spatial beats.

Flexible Waveguides

Derived the coupled fluid–plate dispersion relations and a MATLAB eigensolver for the coupled modes; formulated the nonlinear residual equations for the fluid and structural response, with FEM subroutines under development for numerical validation.

Frequency-Domain Numerical Scheme (FDNS)

Modified FDNS to include mean flow and validated it against Fubini and Blackstock solutions; analytical predictions match FDNS results closely, and large-harmonic simulations resolve resonant cascades leading to shock formation.
